## Phase 1: head grids

- 2: `--draft-grid all --all-slots --clean --apply` in one command: 0.6394 → 0.8603. Worked as documented. `--clean` skipped speckle and last-row passes in all eight views (each would cost 0.007–0.017): on this textured platinum hair the cleanup is useless, as the pitfalls say; the print is one line per view and readable.
- 2: `--all-slots` put chrome slots (`c`, `C`, `l` = chrome base/shade/light) inside the hair and `p` (pants.base) on the face under the fringe: the draft picks by colour only, so greys in the hair land on the chrome ramp. As literals they do not couple (good), but the recipe's legend now says "chrome.light at draft time" for a hair highlight, which misleads a reader; better would be a hint "these cells want a slot on the hair/skin ramp" when an --all-slots letter lands on the head's own hair/skin cells.
- 2: the draft's "N cells have no legend colour within reach (median #756e63): add it and redraft" was useful and actionable: I added `hair.dark` (#7a7064) and then `skin.dark` (#8a6343) as extra slots with legend letters `d`, `z`: +0.0016 and +0.0009. Ramp slots are free-form names (not documented anywhere in the skill; I read `chargen/character.py` to confirm); worth one sentence in the skeleton's palette comment.
- 3/4: the draft reports its second pass per view twice (first and second pass), 16 blocks of output even with `--quiet` for `all`: the grids themselves print. A `--quiet` that drops the grid text when `--apply` writes it would halve the output.
- 4: the rule library's and playbook's snippets write several keys on one line separated by `;` (`type = "grow"; part = ...`): that is not TOML. Pasting them into a --try file crashed tomllib ('Expected newline or end of document', a raw traceback, no hint). Either write the snippets as one key per line or as inline tables, or have --try/recipe loading accept `;` (it cannot: tomllib). The traceback from --try should also say which file and line.
