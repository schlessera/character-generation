Not a bug, but instruments.md's "one row per recipe" reads as if you must pass the base; say "the current recipe is always the first row".
- 4: per-view scoring of each candidate (a small script that appends one snippet to the recipe and prints the per-view deltas) was the method that decided every facing list. The library's Nyx rules were close but not exact on this run: the forearm band at 0.5 lost in the 3/4 fronts and gained in `up_side_l` (library lists `down_side_l`, not `up_side_l`); the pockets' `rows hands top` lost 0.0076 in `down_side_l` (library lists it). - 4: side hem: `edge torso touching legs down` gains in side_l (+0.0036) and loses in side (-0.0016); kept in side_l only.
- 5: `--widths` named every silhouette rule directly: +3/+4 on rows 19-22 in both 3/4 fronts (far shoulder, library n=3 held), -2 on the sleeves from behind (library shrink held), +3/+4 on row 31 in the 3/4 views (the far toe; +0.0002 only). Arbitrary slot names work (`Recipe.color` reads `r[slot]`), which is what made it a two-line fix; the skill never says slots beyond the tone names are allowed. Fix in the tool: `--all-slots` should add NEW slots (on `outline`, `hair`, or a `grid` ramp) for colours no head material has, instead of borrowing body slots; or at least the playbook should say "after --all-slots, rename borrowed body slots before touching those ramps".
